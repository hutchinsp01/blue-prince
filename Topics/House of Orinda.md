# House of Orinda

Historical figures from "History of Orinda (Censored)" ([[Library]]) and the "Orinda Aries stamp" ([[Tomb]]).
Orinda looks like a kingdom and Aries like its ruling line — grouped here rather than as one person each.

- Orinda Aries — stamp found in the Tomb ([[Tomb]])
- Named in the censored history ([[Library]]):
  - Oris
  - Ajera
  - Fenn Aries
  - Arch Aries
  - Eraja
  - King Desilets III
  - War of the Fifth Era
- A suspected uncensored version of this history may exist somewhere ([[Library]])

The [[Erajan Language]] is named for **Eraja** in this list.
