# The Redguard

> [!question] What is the REDGUARD? ([[Shelter]])

What's known:

- Three people stayed down in the [[Shelter]] during the Redguard
- **28 Feb 1987** — [[Randolph Moore]] emails that the RedGuard are coming tomorrow to investigate the disappearance; too risky to move ([[Shelter]])
- **27 Aug 1987** — [[Simon H Sinclair]] orders the [[Blackbridge Grotto]] computer locked down because of the Redguard investigation ([[Laboratory]])
- **31 Aug 1987** — "LOCK IT ALL DOWN" ([[Laboratory]])
- A cousin was smuggled across the **red border** to the southern seafront, 7 months before [[Mary Epsen]] went missing — coincides with the ruby crown theft ([[Shelter]], Red Note VII)
- The insurgent group **RLCF** appears in the [[Archives]]
- [[The Curse of The Black Bridge]] mentions a **BlackGuard** — different to the RedGuard?
