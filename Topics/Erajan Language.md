# Erajan Language

The foreign language on the estate. Taught in the [[Classroom]] at **Grade 8**
(Classroom Rank 7). Named for **Eraja**, one of the names in
[[History of Orinda]] — see [[House of Orinda]].

## Affix pairings

Affixes come in **mirrored pairs** — reverse the letters, invert the meaning.

| Prefix | Meaning | Suffix | Meaning |
|--------|---------|--------|---------|
| AJ | first | JA | last |
| MA | small | AM | large |
| OV | toward | VO | away |
| NEV | new / the start of | VEN | old / the end of |
| EL | I / me\* | LE | a lack of myself\* |

\* When paired with a verb can mean **I will** or **I won't** — similar to **UL / LU**.

## Vocabulary

| Erajan | English |
|--------|---------|
| LOR | word |
| HEW | house |

| NAI | night |
| AREI | future |

## Grammar

> Early Erajan did not use any verbs, so today the rare usage of verbs are
> comprised almost entirely of **loan words** and are mainly used to describe
> physical actions.

That's why English verbs appear untranslated inside otherwise-Erajan text.

## Worked examples

From the Foreign Language Class poster:

- **ajlor** = AJ (first) + LOR (word) → "first word"
- **nevdau** = NEV (start of) + DAU (day) → "dawn"
- **Ulgo?** = UL (will) + *go* (loan-word verb) → "will go?"

## Untranslated text found

### The letter

Full artefact: [[Erajan Letter]] — found at Clara Epsen's grave in the [[Tomb]].

```
Oreldajiris,
Ulelddau, uleldnai, uleldmora.
Orulelgift eldhew oriss. Nerdau uldareile.
Nerdau eldareilu. Ajelbid ulheed eldlor: Uloddiris.
Hew sinclairlu. Hew epsenlu. . . Forget uldein inn.
Uldjorjeari.. Uldarei orissfennro. Uldeinjororo.
Oreraja. Oriss nerarei.

                    Lorja eldiris,
                              Uldjora
```

Hooks visible without full translation:

- **`hew`** = house. `Hew sinclairlu` / `Hew epsenlu` — House of [[Herbert of Sinclair|Sinclair]], House of [[Mary Epsen|Epsen]]
- **`Forget`** — an English loan-word verb, exactly as the grammar note predicts
- **`dau`** / **`nai`** in `Ulelddau, uleldnai` — day and night
- **`arei`** = future, in `uldarei` and `eldareilu`
- Signed **Uldjora**; opens `Oreldajiris,` and closes `Lorja eldiris,`

## Grade 8 material

The worksheet asks for six English→Erajan translations:

1. "Hello, can you teach me a new word?"
2. "The large house is toward the east."
3. "I will swim at dawn."
4. "I won't read at night."
5. "The branches of the tree are old."
6. "The trunk of the tree is small."
