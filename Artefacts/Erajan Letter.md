# Erajan Letter

Written in [[Erajan Language|Erajan]]. Names both House Sinclair and House
Epsen (`hew sinclairlu`, `hew epsenlu`), carries one untranslated English
loan-word verb ("Forget"), and is signed **Uldjora**.
